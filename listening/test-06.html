Speaker 8 Today, we’ll be looking at how the town of Bridlington has evolved over time, economically, socially, and structurally. And we’ll also explore some of the initiatives that have been proposed for its continued growth. Let’s begin with the town’s economic background. Historically, Bridlington relied heavily on manufacturing. One particular trade that stood out was the production of furniture, chairs, tables, wardrobes, and other wooden household goods.This craft developed into a full-scale industry that, for decades, defined the town’s identity and served as a major employment hub. In fact, it was once the most prominent sector within the town’s economy. However, over the years, industrial decline paired with globalization has shifted the economic base of the town. What we see now is an economy that thrives not on manual labor, but on intangible services. The sector, including financial consultancy, customer support, and digital marketing, has risen rapidly. This area now surpasses traditional manufacturing in terms of revenue generation and job creation. In fact, numerous insurance firms that offer coverage for accidents, health, and property have set up local offices here, and these companies have brought in a wide range of positions, from entry-level call center staff to data analysts and IT specialists. This has significantly helped in reducing unemployment figures in the region. Now, turning to community development. One problem that’s been observed regularly, especially during term time, is the traffic buildup in residential zones. The cause? Parents stopping to gather their children in areas located just meters away from educational institutions. The resulting congestion during morning and afternoon school runs has become a real concern for urban planners. To accommodate the town’s growing population, the municipal government has proposed an extensive residential expansion project. According to official estimates, the town will require upwards of 16,000 new dwellings within the next decade. And to support this population boom, it’s expected that around 20,000 jobs will also be created across various sectors, both public and private. In the realm of education, plans have also been laid out to support higher learning. The local authority is collaborating with a well-known regional university to construct a dedicated campus for advanced study. This new institution, which will include accommodation, lecture halls, and research centers, is expected to draw not only domestic students, but international scholars as well. Next, let’s address the town’s transport strategy. As part of the wider sustainability plan, Bridlington aims to reduce the flow of motor vehicles across its center. The target is to lower the number of cars on the road by approximately one-fifth. A bold figure, but one that town officials believe is achievable through a combination of incentives and awareness campaigns. Tackling traffic effectively will require community-wide cooperation. Encouraging eco-friendly modes of transport is also central to this vision. There has been a notable push to promote cycling, particularly for shorter commutes. Expanded bike paths, upgraded safety measures, and even reward schemes for daily cyclists are all part of the agenda. Finally, let’s look at how the use of commercial space is expected to shift. With the increasing trend towards online shopping and digital marketplaces, the demand for high street shop fronts has diminished. As a result, less emphasis will be placed on space allocated to retail. Instead, approximately 80,000 square meters of space in and around the town center have been earmarked for a different purpose. The focus will shift to flexible working environments, spaces that can accommodate small companies, freelancers, and digital startups. In other words, future construction will prioritize office use and rental business spaces over traditional stores.
